= Blood Pressure -- ANF and Complex Concept Fixture

The first test document for interactive ANF statement and complex concept work.
It pairs a clinical narrative with the statements a lift of it should produce,
and with the complex concept those statements promote to and expand from.
Nothing clinical here is new: every value comes from the sources below,
and every place this document departs from them says so.

== Sources

* Narrative and statements 1 to 7: kompendium topic `anf-example-blood-pressure`
  (`knowledge-kompendium/anf`, `topics/src/docs/asciidoc/topics/anf/example-blood-pressure.adoc`).
* Complex concept: ike-lab-documents topic `anf-complex-concept-examples`,
  Example 1, Sitting Systolic Blood Pressure (Focused)
  (`topics/src/docs/asciidoc/topics/anf/complex-concept-examples.adoc`).

== Conventions

* Each `[anf]` block is in the `AnfAdoc` grammar.
  A grounded value names its concept by the SCTID the source gives, in `@id`;
  the key and the label in `k:Key[label]` are human aids the codec does not trust.
* A concept the source names with no identifier is a `candidate[...]` value:
  a clear meaning the knowledge base does not carry yet.
  Where a lifted statement's topic is the nearest existing concept, `near=` names it;
  that pointer is this fixture's, not the source's.
* `status::` is the codec's key for the performance's disposition attribute.
* Presence is `[1,1]` on the Presence scale, SCTID 705057003; absence is `[0,0]`.
* The `[anf]` record carries the topic, the subject of information and the circumstance.
  The subject of record, the authors, the participants and the associated statements
  are beyond it, so each section lists them in a table beside the block.

[#open-questions]
== Open Questions the Sources Leave

. Statement 6 has no table in the kompendium; its result is derived (see the statement).
. The complex concept's focus value is `[120, 121)` mmHg; the kompendium's statement 3 is `[120, 120]`.
  The two sources disagree on how a provided whole number is written.
. The complex concept splits "sitting" into a position and a duration;
  the kompendium has one statement whose topic is the position and whose result is the duration.
. "Recent voiding" is presence with no window; the narrative and statement 6 carry "not more than 30 minutes".
. "Systolic blood pressure observation" is identified here with SCTID 271649006, the kompendium's systolic topic.
. The narrative's body site (right brachial artery) and device (adult BP cuff, home BP cuff)
  are in none of the kompendium's statements, so they are not here either.
. The repetition's period start is written as a duration, `1 Week`,
  where the request control topic defines it as a time stamp;
  the frequency's unit is the week SCTID, a time unit, for a rate per week.
. The kompendium names no association type for statements 5 and 6.
  The complex concept says precondition,
  but the starter set's statement associations have no precondition type.
. The ike-lab-documents copy of the blood pressure example differs:
  statement 5's result is `[-5, 0] Minute`,
  and statement 4's topic is 271649006, the systolic SCTID.
  This fixture follows the kompendium.
