= PERRLA -- ANF and Complex Concept Fixture

A test document for interactive ANF statement and complex concept work.
It pairs two PERRLA narratives with the statements a lift of each should produce,
and with the complex concept PERRLA expands into.
Nothing clinical here is new: every value comes from the sources below,
and every place this document departs from them says so.

== Sources

* Narratives and statements: kompendium topic `anf-example-bilateral-assessment`
  (`knowledge-kompendium/anf`, `topics/src/docs/asciidoc/topics/anf/example-bilateral-assessment.adoc`),
  Example 1, PERRLA -- Pupils Equal (Qualitative), and Example 2, PERRLA -- Pupils Equal (Quantitative).
* Complex concept: ike-lab-documents topic `anf-complex-concept-examples`,
  Example 2, PERRLA (Uniform AND)
  (`topics/src/docs/asciidoc/topics/anf/complex-concept-examples.adoc`).

== Conventions

The conventions are those of `blood-pressure.adoc`:
`@id` is the SCTID the source gives,
a concept with no identifier is a `candidate[...]` value,
`status::` is the codec's key for the disposition,
and presence is `[1,1]` on the Presence scale, SCTID 705057003.
The kompendium places the pupil in the context as `location`;
the performance's attribute for it is the body site, `body_site::`.

which carries its measurement basis (the contralateral anatomical structure).

== Expected Complex Concept: PERRLA

A uniform AND expansion: the provided value distributes to every member.

=== Clinical Input

The statement a promotion produces, and the one an expansion starts from.
The source's input is the code "PERRLA assessment" with the value `[1, 1]` (present/true -- all normal).

[anf#perrla-cc-input,type=performance]

result:: [1,1] k:Presence[Presence] @id=705057003
--

[#open-questions]
== Open Questions the Sources Leave

. Is "symmetric" presence on the Presence scale, or a value on the comparative unit?
  The kompendium writes it on the comparative unit; the structural definition puts presence on the Presence scale.
  This fixture keeps the comparative unit and corrects only the interval.
. For the narrative "PERRLA", the kompendium's lift isolates one element, pupils equal,
  while the complex concept treats PERRLA as a compound code that expands to four members.
  Which one a lift should produce, or whether it should produce the compound and offer the expansion, is open.
. Equality is a pupil diameter statement on a comparative unit in the kompendium,
  and a "Pupil equality assessment" topic on presence in the complex concept.
. Only pupil equality has kompendium statements; roundness, light reactivity and accommodation have none.
. The kompendium places the pupil as `location` in the context; this fixture writes it as the body site.
. Example 1's `semantic` row is the result's measure semantic; Example 2's `semantic` rows repeat the unit.
. The member association "Component" has no concept, and the complex concept builder's members carry no association.
