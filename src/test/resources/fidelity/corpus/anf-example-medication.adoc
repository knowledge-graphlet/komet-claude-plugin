= Medication History Clinical Narrative -- ANF Worked Example

indexterm:[ANF, medication history example]
This reference provides a complete worked example of building
ANF statements from a medication history clinical narrative.
Ten ANF statements are derived from a single encounter,
covering demographics (gender and age),
diagnoses (hypertension and pre-diabetes),
current medications (lisinopril and metformin),
medication adherence,
adverse drug reactions,
prior medications (hydrochlorothiazide),
and medication side effects.
Each statement is presented in compact form showing only the
populated attributes.

== Clinical Narrative

indexterm:[clinical narrative, medication history]
The following clinical narrative serves as the source for all
ten ANF statements in this example.

[example]
====
*Medication History* +
Patient Name:
Jane Doe +
Patient Identifier:
5486-89754 +
Date of Encounter:
January 5,
2025 +
Clinician:
Dr. Jim Wilson (ID# 24-6759)

Patient is a 58-year old female with a history of hypertension
and pre-diabetes.
She is currently taking lisinopril,
20 mg oral tablet,
once daily for blood pressure control,
which she has been on for the past 2 years with good adherence
and tolerability.
Prior to starting lisinopril,
she was briefly trialed on hydrochlorothiazide 25 mg oral tablet,
once daily,
but experienced mild dizziness and was switched due to side effects.
The patient takes metformin 500 mg oral tablet,
twice daily for glucose control which she has been on for the past 6 months.
====
