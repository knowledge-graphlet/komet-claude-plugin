[[anf-polarity-states]]
= Absent, Indeterminate, and Not Sought

A narrative that mentions a finding says one of four things:
it was looked for and found,
looked for and not found,
looked for without a usable answer,
or never looked for.
ANF keeps the four apart with two Performance Circumstance
attributes, the Result and the Disposition
(xref:anf-performance-result[Performance Circumstance Results]).
An independent verification study of ten eye-exam narratives
(2026) found polarity the least reproducible element across
human and agent encoders (kappa 0.28, 73 negation flips),
while examination status and laterality agreed almost perfectly.

== The Four States

indexterm:[polarity, four states]
The topic never carries polarity.
"No afferent pupillary defect" has the topic
_relative afferent pupillary defect_ and a result of absent;
the ((Presence scale)) is what makes absence a value
rather than a negated concept.

.The four states and where each is recorded
[cols="2,2,1", options="header"]
|===
| The record says | Result | Disposition

| Assessed and present
| `[1, 1]` on the Presence scale
| performed

| Assessed and absent
| `[0, 0]` on the Presence scale
| performed

| Assessed, indeterminate
| `[0, 1]` on the Presence scale
| performed

| Not done, or deferred
| none
| not sought
|===

indexterm:[indeterminate result, Presence scale]
An ((indeterminate)) result --
a fundus view obscured by a dense cataract,
a visual field too unreliable to read --
is still a result:
the act was performed and yielded no answer;
the structural definition writes it `[0, 1]`.

indexterm:[disposition, not sought]
When the record states that an element was not done
or was deferred,
write a statement with disposition "not sought" and no result.
When the record is simply silent,
write nothing:
ANF documents what was stated, not what was omitted
(xref:anf-missing-data[Missing Identifiers, Dates, and Time Zones]).

== Hedged Negatives

A ((hedged negative)) --
"none visible," "no clear," "without obvious" --
asserts absence.
Encode it `[0, 0]`.
The hedge speaks to the method or the view,
not to the determination:
record the method where the narrative names one
and keep the wording as provenance
(xref:anf-source-fidelity[Source Fidelity Without a Qualifier Field]).
Never promote a hedged negative to indeterminate.

== Considered, Suspected, and Working Diagnoses

indexterm:[differential diagnosis, ANF encoding]
A finding that is merely considered,
or that appears in a differential,
has been asserted neither way.
Never encode it as absent.
If the reasoning matters,
capture it as a
xref:anf-narrative-circumstance[Narrative Circumstance];
otherwise omit it.

indexterm:[suspected diagnosis, encoded as present]
A suspected, probable, or working diagnosis is different:
it is an affirmative assertion made with stated confidence.
Encode it present.
Indeterminate means the assessment produced no answer;
a suspected diagnosis is an answer offered provisionally.

NOTE: The model has no attribute for epistemic confidence;
the 2026 appendix held "suspected" and "probable" in a
template qualifier ANF does not have.
Candidates are an association to a Narrative statement
carrying the wording, or a future attribute.
Until that is settled, keep the wording as provenance.

NOTE: This guide's worked examples still show the older
Countable Quantity form:
`[1, INF)` true, `[0, 0]` false, `[0, INF)` unknown.
The Presence-scale form is the model's current form;
the two agree on polarity and differ in the measure semantic.
