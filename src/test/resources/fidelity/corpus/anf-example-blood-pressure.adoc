= Blood Pressure Clinical Narrative -- ANF Worked Example

indexterm:[ANF, blood pressure example]
This reference provides a complete worked example of building
ANF statements from a blood pressure clinical narrative.
Seven ANF statements are derived from a single encounter,
covering demographics (gender and age),
vital sign measurements (systolic and diastolic blood pressure),
associated circumstantial statements (sitting position and
micturition finding),
and a request for home blood pressure monitoring.
Each statement is presented in compact form showing only the
populated attributes.

== Clinical Narrative

indexterm:[clinical narrative, blood pressure]
The following clinical narrative serves as the source for all
seven ANF statements in this example.

[example]
====
*Blood Pressure* +
Patient Name:
James Doe +
Patient Identifier:
6594-86214 +
Date of Encounter:
August 15,
2024 +
Clinician:
Dr. Hannah Smith (ID# 47-3659)

A 62-year-old man presented to his doctor's office for routine checkup.
He had a Systolic Blood Pressure of 120 mmHg,
and a Diastolic Blood Pressure of 85 mmHg,
taken on Right Brachial Artery,
using BP cuff adult size.
Patient in sitting position for at least 5 minutes,
urinated not more than 30 minutes prior to measurement.
The patient was asked to take his blood pressure weekly at home
for 4 weeks using home blood pressure cuff.
====

== Isolation of Clinical Elements

indexterm:[clinical element isolation, blood pressure]
The first step is to identify the key clinical elements from
which the ANF statements will be created.
Seven ANF statements result from this narrative.
The final two statements regarding sitting and urination are
associated statements linked to the blood pressure measurements,
as they do not stand alone clinically.

.Clinical Elements for Blood Pressure Use Case
[cols="2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Clinical Element | ANF Statement Topic

| Gender (male)
| Male

| 62 years old
| Chronological Age

| Systolic BP
| Systolic BP

| Diastolic BP
| Diastolic BP

| Sitting for 5 minutes
| Sitting Position

| Urinated, 30 min ago
| Micturition Finding

| Blood Pressure Request
| Blood Pressure Procedure
|===

For each statement below,
SNOMED CT codes are used when available.
Codes are accurate as of July 31,
2025. When no appropriate SNOMED CT code exists,
a generated public identifier is used.

== Statement 6: Micturition Finding

indexterm:[ANF, associated statement, micturition]
This associated statement records that the patient urinated not
more than 30 minutes prior to the blood pressure measurement.
Like the sitting position statement,
this provides contextual information relevant to interpreting
the blood pressure results.

NOTE: The Micturition Finding statement follows the same structural pattern as the Sitting Position statement.
It is linked to both the Systolic BP and Diastolic BP statements through the associated statement mechanism.
