= Narrative Circumstance

indexterm:[ANF, narrative circumstance]
The ((Narrative Circumstance)) is a fallback mechanism within
ANF for clinical information that cannot be captured in the
structured Performance or Request data elements.
It preserves clinically relevant detail as free text (String)
when the structured model lacks the expressiveness to encode
the information without losing nuance.
The Narrative Circumstance is intentionally not meant to be
used frequently --
the vast majority of clinical statements should use Performance
or Request Circumstances.

Three categories of clinical information commonly require the
Narrative Circumstance:

indexterm:[narrative circumstance, contextual detail]
*Contextual detail not tied to a codable act.* Some clinical
detail is important but does not fit into a standard code.
For example,
ANF statements may capture that a patient has a high fever,
severe headaches,
and nausea,
but they cannot capture the fact that the patient recently
traveled to a region with a high incidence of Dengue Fever.
This travel context can be preserved in an associated Narrative
Circumstance statement.

indexterm:[narrative circumstance, patient-reported description]
*Patient-reported descriptions.* Vague or subjective patient
descriptions are common in clinical encounters.
Although imprecise,
these descriptions may be critical for understanding symptoms
or guiding differential diagnosis.
For example,
a patient may describe feeling like "something is crawling
under their skin," or describe a headache as "different from
their usual headaches."
Such descriptions are captured as narrative text.

indexterm:[narrative circumstance, historical timeline]
*Historical narratives or complex timelines.* Some clinically
relevant detail involves timelines that are difficult to
represent fully in the structured ANF model without losing nuance.
For example,
a patient may report episodes of palpitations that began
shortly after taking on caregiving responsibilities for her mother --
a temporal correlation that may be important for differential
diagnosis to rule out cardiac issues,
but that does not reduce cleanly to structured Circumstance attributes.

NOTE: The Narrative Circumstance is not a general-purpose comment field.
It should be used only when the clinical information genuinely cannot be expressed through the structured Performance or Request models.
An ANF statement with a ((BRCA1)) gene mutation as its Topic, for instance, might use a Narrative Circumstance to record the exact mutation detail that falls outside the codable structure.
